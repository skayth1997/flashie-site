Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no email. Your photos and history stay on your iPhone.

Are the filters really free?

Yes. The Filters tab (warm and cool film, 35mm grain, digicam flash, B&W, plus beauty and adjust tools) works on any photo, right on your iPhone, with no limits.

How do AI photos and credits work?

Your first AI photo is free. For more, choose a weekly plan with new photos every day (Plus: 5 a day, Gold: 10 a day), or buy a credit pack once: one credit is one photo, and credits never expire.

Will it still look like me?

Yes. Flashie is tuned to keep your face. With "Keep my face" you choose how closely: Subtle, Normal or Strong, where Strong keeps your exact features and only changes light, color and background.

What happens to my selfie?

It's sent to Google's Gemini AI to create your photo and isn't stored by Flashie. For ages 16 and up.
